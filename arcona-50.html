The 50

Built for the
life you want.

"The original 465 was already one of the finest performance cruisers ever built. The Mk II takes everything that made her beloved and makes it better — without losing what made it special."

— Arcona Design Team

The Arcona 50 is a different kind of boat. Where the rest of the range is built for the race course as much as the cruising ground, the 50 is designed around the life you want to live on the water — long summers, warm anchorages, evenings that stretch on too long.

A spacious sunbed forward, perfect for reading with a cocktail in hand. Twin aft benches that conceal an outdoor kitchenette and integrated fridge, ready for entertaining in any port from Palma to Portofino. The cockpit is designed for living, not just sailing — L-shaped benches, two tables, and room for everyone.

Below, the owner's cabin spans the full beam with a king-size island bed and en-suite shower. Natural light floods in through hull windows and flush deck hatches. Oak or mahogany, built on Orust. Every detail considered, nothing overdone.

What Makes Her

Three reasons
sailors choose the Arcona 50.

01
Al Fresco Living
Two versatile aft cockpit benches conceal an outdoor kitchenette and integrated fridge — the perfect setup for long evenings in harbour. A spacious foredeck sunbed invites you to stay. The entire deck is a platform for life outdoors.
02
The Owner's Cabin
A king-size island bed, en-suite with separate shower, large hull windows, and a forward-facing coachroof window. It is the kind of space that makes you want to stay at anchor for one more day. Two double guest cabins aft complete the picture.
03
Ocean Capable
Beneath the lifestyle, the bones of a serious offshore yacht. CE Category A. Twin rudders, three-spreader mast, 385L fuel, 375L water. Sail her to the Med, then live on her once you arrive. She is equally at home on both sides of the passage.
