03

Interior Wood

Two interior finishes across the range — Khaya Mahogany and Light Oak. Both are hand-varnished to a satin finish by our craftsmen in Henån.

Khaya Mahogany
Rich, warm reddish tones. The classic Arcona interior. High-build satin varnish.
Light Oak
Bright, airy Scandinavian light oak. A lighter, contemporary alternative.

Interior finish is selected at order. Our craftsmen will apply as many coats of varnish as it takes to meet our standards — we will varnish until we are satisfied.
