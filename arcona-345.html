The 345

Small boat sailing,
no compromises.

"Ideal for shorthanded sailing, the Arcona 345 is a joy to helm — a new favourite performance cruiser on the regatta circuit and for cruising with family and friends."

— Arcona Yachts

The Arcona 345 packs everything that defines the range into 34 feet — a tall, efficient fractional rig, a vacuum infused hull with 20mm Divinycell core, twin wheels as standard, and an open transom. Wide side decks and a spacious cockpit make her extremely easy to handle, whether racing or cruising with family.

Below, the separate shower heads is a rare feature at this size — a proper heads compartment with its own shower, forward cabin, and one aft double cabin. The saloon seats six comfortably at the twin-leaf table. Khaya Mahogany or Light Oak throughout, varnished to a satin finish.

Built on Orust, to the same standard as every Arcona before her.

What Makes Her

Three reasons
sailors choose the Arcona 345.

01
Twin Wheels Standard
Twin wheels as standard on a 34-footer — letting you sit well to windward with a clear view of the sails. The spacious cockpit, wide side decks and open transom make her extremely easy to handle under sail.
02
Separate Shower Heads
A separate shower compartment in the heads is a rare feature at 34 feet — and one that makes a real difference on longer passages. Forward owner's cabin, one aft double, and a saloon that seats six at the twin-leaf table.
03
Regatta Ready
Prepped for spinnaker equipment, keel-stepped mast, and twin spreader rig. All sheets and halyards led aft to the cockpit. CE Category A — Ocean. A natural on the regatta circuit, equally at home on a family coastal cruise.
