The Arcona DNA

Not a racer.
Not a cruiser.
Both.

The hardest yacht to design is the one that does everything well. A pure racer ignores comfort. A pure cruiser ignores speed. Arcona has spent 38 years refusing that compromise.

Every hull we build starts with a single obsession: the lightest possible platform that still feels like home. Weight is performance. Weight is safety. Weight is the reason you arrive first and arrive well.

Philosophy
"It is a fantastic
feeling when she
takes off."

Torgny Jansson founded Arcona with a conviction: a yacht that goes fast should also feel safe, beautiful, and liveable. That belief hasn't changed.

Every design decision — hull shape, keel geometry, interior layout — is tested against a simple question: does this make the boat better for both the racer and the cruiser?

Built on the island of Orust, where Sweden's boat-building tradition runs deepest.
