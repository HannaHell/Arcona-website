The Story

Familiar soul,
sharper edge.

"The original 465 was already one of the finest performance cruisers ever built. The Mk II takes everything that made her beloved and makes it better — without losing what made it special."

— Arcona Design Team

The Arcona 465 earned its reputation the hard way — on open water, in real conditions, sailed by people who expected everything from a 46-footer. Fast passages. Comfortable nights. A helm that rewards attention without punishing mistakes.

The Mk II starts from that inheritance and asks harder questions. A revised hull form pushes the performance envelope further, with a wider stern and refined underwater sections that translate into better upwind VMG and a more planted feel in a seaway. The rig has been rebalanced to work harder off the wind, where blue-water passages are won.

Below, the interior is a complete redesign — three finish options, two panel treatments, and a layout that gives the saloon more light and the cabins more privacy. Built on Orust, as always. The craftsmen are the same. So is the standard.

What Makes Her

Three reasons
sailors choose the 465 Mk II.

01
Evolved Hull
The Mk II hull is not a facelift. Wider aft sections increase form stability and downwind speed. Refined underwater lines improve VMG upwind. The result is a boat that is measurably faster than her predecessor in every condition.
02
The Interior
Three finishes, two panel treatments — oak, mahogany, or oak with dark contrast accents. Kläddaskott for softness and warmth, träskott for a more classic Scandinavian feel. Every combination is hand-built on Orust and finished to the same exacting standard.
03
Offshore Ready
CE Category A — Ocean. A deep, well-protected cockpit. Self-tacking jib option. A rig designed for short-handed sailing. The 465 Mk II is as comfortable on a North Sea passage as it is in an Archipelago race.
