The Yard

Service by the
people who
built your boat.

Orust Yacht Service — OYS — is part of the Najad Group, operating from the same yard in Henån where Arcona yachts are produced. With over 300 boat spaces, two cranes handling up to 40 tonnes, and craftsmen who have spent careers working on performance sailing yachts, it is one of northern Europe's most capable service operations.

For Arcona owners, this means your yacht comes back to the people who know it inside out. The same hands that built it can service it, repair it, and prepare it for whatever you have planned next.

What We Offer

Full care,
one location.

01 — Storage
Winter Storage
Store your Arcona safely and securely in our modern, climate-controlled storage halls — tempered and dehumidified. Indoor and outdoor options available, with launch and recovery handled in-house.
02 — Maintenance
Service & Repair
Annual service, osmosis treatment, antifouling, engine maintenance, teak renewal and full hull repairs. Composite specialists, woodworkers, engine technicians and riggers — all under one roof.
03 — Rig
Rig & Sails
Full rigging services including rig inspections, standing rigging replacement, mast work and sail repairs. A dedicated rigging hall and sail dryer on site. We prepare rigs for offshore passages and racing alike.
04 — Electronics
Installation & Equipment
Anchor winches, electric winches, navigation and instrument installation, solar panels, battery and energy solutions. Full electrical upgrades and new systems fitted to your specification.
05 — Insurance
Insurance Claims
Experienced in handling all types of insurance cases — groundings, collisions, rig damage and water ingress. We work directly with insurers and have handled over 50 cases per season in recent years.
06 — Parts
Spare Parts
A marine spare parts shop on site, stocked with essentials for Arcona and other performance yachts. We can also source and supply specific parts for your model — contact us with your requirements.

One of the advantages of buying an Arcona directly from the yard is that service stays simple. You know exactly where your boat is, who is working on it, and what has been done. No intermediaries, no guesswork.

We can arrange transportation of your yacht to and from Henån if you are not local — and for owners planning longer passages or offshore adventures, we offer preparation packages tailored to your itinerary.
