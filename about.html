Our Story

Built for
performance,
shaped by sailors.

Arcona's roots lie in Swedish boatbuilding, beginning as a small family yard in the Stockholm archipelago in the late 1960s. From the very beginning, Arcona has been about more than building yachts — it has been about sharing a way of sailing. Owners, sailors and builders have always been closely connected, united by a belief in performance, balance and long-term quality.

That philosophy took form in 1982 with the Arcona 32, defining the Arcona approach: a racer's heart paired with a cruiser's comfort. A long-standing collaboration with designer Stefan Qviberg shaped the brand's distinctive balance and sailing feel, supported by Arcona's signature galvanised steel frame, known for its strength, stiffness and confidence under sail.

Through the 2000s, models such as the Arcona 400 established the modern Arcona look, while advanced construction methods reduced weight and increased strength. International recognition followed, including European Yacht of the Year for the Arcona 430 in 2009 — an achievement shared not only by the yard, but by the growing community of Arcona owners sailing their yachts across the world.

Since 2018, Arcona has been part of Najad Group, retaining its clear focus on performance-driven design. This continued evolution has led to further accolades, including European Yacht of the Year for both the Arcona 435 and the Arcona 50 — the latter marking Arcona's entry into the super-premium segment.

Today, choosing an Arcona is not simply about owning a yacht. It is about becoming part of a long-standing family of sailors — people who value craftsmanship, sailing performance, and the quiet confidence that comes from a boat built by those who truly sail themselves.

Design Philosophy

The
Arcona
DNA

"There is a particular moment when an Arcona comes alive and accelerates with balance and precision. The sensation is immediate, and it's a feeling shaped by experience."

Arcona is a yacht built by sailors: we notice when the reach to a control line is just a little too far, when a foothold could be better placed, or when something could be made simpler. These insights are distilled into all design decisions, resulting in yachts that feel intuitive, composed, and purpose-built.

At the heart of our philosophy is the performance cruiser: a yacht that is fast and efficient yet remains comfortable and safe for family cruising. Achieving this balance is the real challenge, and the foundation of every Arcona design.

Weight is key. A lighter yacht is faster and more responsive, while allowing capacity for cruising equipment without compromise. Combined with carefully considered cockpit layouts, every Arcona is designed to be confidently sailed shorthanded in any conditions.

All Arcona models are modern boats, with efficient sail plans — tall, powerful and easily managed — unmistakably Arcona at sea.

Swedish Quality

In the
Details

Building a yacht involves thousands of decisions, each one influencing how the boat feels, sails and endures over time. At Arcona, every choice is guided by a single question: does this meet our standards?

The hull is built to be stiff, strong and safe, forming the backbone of a yacht designed for long-term ownership and confident sailing. Swedish craftsmanship and design runs through the build, the structure and the clean, purposeful lines of every Arcona.

Our yachts are airy, functional and carefully detailed, with qualities that reveal themselves gradually, over years of use. These are boats built to age well, both technically and aesthetically.

Quite simply, we build yachts we are proud of, and yachts we believe you will continue to appreciate, season after season.
