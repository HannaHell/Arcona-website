The Story

Speed without
compromise.

"The 435 Mk II is the most complete performance cruiser we have ever tested. Nothing comes close."

— European Yacht of the Year 2019 Jury

The Arcona 435 Mk II didn't just win Yacht of the Year. It redefined what a performance cruiser can be. Lighter, stiffer, faster than its predecessor — but with the same commitment to life aboard that Arcona has always carried.

The hull is pure performance: a fine entry, flat sections aft, a deep fin keel that generates lift under every point of sail. Push her and she accelerates with a snap that surprises even experienced sailors. Ease off and she settles into a comfortable, confidence-inspiring cruise.

Below, the interior is a testament to Swedish craftsmanship. Teak and oak joinery, clean sightlines, a galley designed for passage making. Not a boat to be admired at the dock — a boat to be sailed.

What Makes Her

Three reasons
sailors choose the 435 Mk II.

01
Weight Obsession
Every kilo matters. Arcona's construction philosophy starts with removing weight before adding features. The result is a hull-to-displacement ratio that makes other 43-footers feel sluggish.
02
Swedish Joinery
Below decks, the 435 Mk II is as refined as any boat in her class. Teak and oak, clean geometry, considered storage. Built on Orust by people who sail.
03
The Helm
Two wheels, excellent visibility, a traveller positioned for single-handed control. Designed to be sailed by two across an ocean and raced by four on a Saturday afternoon.
